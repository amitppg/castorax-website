Castor Oil Grades and Specialty Oils

Explore the listed castor-oil grades and selected specialty oils. Product suitability and supporting documents depend on the grade and intended application.

Castor Oil IP GradeCastor oil grade identified as I.P.; confirm applicable requirements and batch documents. Castor Oil BSS GradeBSS trade grade with batch values confirmed against the buyer’s specification. Commercial Grade Castor OilIndustrial castor oil supplied against agreed process and quality requirements. Castor Oil FSGFirst Special Grade castor oil; request current batch specification for review. Cosmetic Grade Castor OilCastor oil for personal-care formulations; confirm grade and formulation requirements. Pale Pressed Grade (PPG) Castor OilTrade grade generally selected where a lighter-coloured castor oil is required. Hydrogenated Castor OilCastor wax used as a structuring or thickening ingredient in formulated products. Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO)Modified castor oil used in coatings, paints, varnishes, alkyd resins and inks. Turkey Red Oil (TRO)Sulfated castor-oil derivative used for wetting or emulsifying applications. Neem OilCold-pressed oil produced from seeds collected from wild-growing neem trees. Liquid Paraffin (White Oil)Refined liquid hydrocarbon mixture available in grades and viscosity ranges. Glycerin (Glycerol)Glycerol (C₃H₈O₃), used as a humectant, solvent and plasticizer. Eucalyptus OilDistilled essential oil; cineole profile depends on species and grade.
