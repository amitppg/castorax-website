Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products and services

What is pharmaceutical grade castor oil?

+

A pharmaceutical-grade raw material must meet the standard and purchase specification required by the buyer. The Castor Oil IP Grade page shows selected limits from the supplied IP-grade sheet; this does not establish USP or BP compliance. Request the current complete specification and lot-specific COA for review.

Where is Simmi Enterprises located?

+

Simmi Enterprises is based in Bhayander East, Mumbai. Buyers can share their required castor-oil grade, application, quantity, and delivery location to check current supply options.

What is the difference between USP, BP, and IP castor oil?

+

USP, BP, and IP are separate pharmacopoeial standards. A product should only be described as meeting a particular standard when its current specification and batch documents support that claim. The Castor Oil IP Grade page currently lists selected IP-grade limits; ask us to confirm any additional standard required for your order.

What certifications does Castorax have?

+

Licences and product documents depend on the product and source. Please contact us to confirm which current licences, certificates, and lot documents apply to the specific product you are considering.

What products does Castorax supply?

+

The current catalogue lists 13 products: Castor Oil IP Grade, Castor Oil BSS Grade, Commercial Grade Castor Oil, Castor Oil FSG, Cosmetic Grade Castor Oil, Pale Pressed Grade (PPG) Castor Oil, Hydrogenated Castor Oil, Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO), Turkey Red Oil (TRO), Neem Oil, Liquid Paraffin, Glycerin, and Eucalyptus Oil. Grade availability and supporting documents should be confirmed for each enquiry.

What is the minimum order quantity for castor oil?

+

Minimum order quantities and packaging depend on the product, grade, and available lot. Share the product, target quantity, packaging preference, and delivery location so we can confirm the current options.

Does Castorax export castor oil internationally?

+

We can review export enquiries based on the product and destination. Availability, required documents, and applicable standards vary by grade and country, so please provide the destination and product for confirmation.

What is the shelf life of castor oil?

+

Shelf life and storage guidance vary by product, grade, source, and packaging. Use the information on the current product specification or lot documents, and confirm it for the material being quoted.

What is hydrogenated castor oil used for?

+

Hydrogenated castor oil, also known as castor wax, is used as a structuring or thickening ingredient in formulations such as greases, coatings, polishes, and personal-care products. Confirm the required physical limits and suitability against the current product specification and batch documents.

How do I verify castor oil quality?

+

Compare the offered grade with your current purchase specification and review a batch-specific COA, including test methods and units. Ask the supplier to clarify any missing or unclear parameter and confirm that the documents correspond to the quoted lot.

What documentation do you provide with shipments?

+

Documents vary by product, grade, source, and destination. Ask which current documents are available for the specific material and lot being quoted; these may include a COA or SDS where applicable.

How quickly can you supply castor oil?

+

Lead time depends on product availability, quantity, packaging, and delivery destination. Share these details in your enquiry so we can confirm a realistic schedule with the quotation.
